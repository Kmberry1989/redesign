About the Kokomo Art Association

Since 1926, the Kokomo Art Association (KAA) has championed visual arts education, exhibition, and community engagement throughout north‑central Indiana.

Our Mission

To connect, educate, and inspire through the visual arts, ensuring art is accessible to all in our community.

Centennial Timeline

  • 1926: First informal gathering of local artists; KAA founded.
  • 1950s–1970s: Regular juried shows, school outreach, and traveling exhibits.
  • 2014: Artist Alley opens in downtown Kokomo.
  • 2025: 12th Annual Artist Alley project and centennial celebrations underway.

Impact by the Numbers (2024)

  • 📈 4,200 gallery visitors
  • 🎨 630 class enrollments
  • 🖼️ 95 regional artists exhibited
